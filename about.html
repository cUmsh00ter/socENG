about us


At Cyberoxytocin, we bridge the worlds of cybersecurity and human psychology to address one of the most powerful yet overlooked attack vectors: social engneering. Our specialled engineering program examines how trust, rapport and emotional connection are leveraged in digital environments to influence decisions and bypass technical defenses. Through research-driven, ethical simulations, and awareness iniatives, we intend to equip individuals and organizations with the knowledge and practical skills to recognize, resist, and ethically navigate these human-centered tactics. Our core intentions to advocate for the importance of privacy and security.
